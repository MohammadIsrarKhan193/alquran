Privacy Policy

Quran Karim by MÎK Platforms  •  Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short: Quran Karim has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your reading progress, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone. The app uses your location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and only while you use those features.

1. Who we are

Quran Karim (the "App") is published by MÎK Platforms ("we", "us"). Contact: mikplatforms@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data. We do not show advertising and we do not sell or share personal data for marketing.

3. Information the App uses

Location (optional). If you allow location access, the App uses your device location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Location is requested only while the App is open (foreground). We do not use background location and we do not keep a location history on any server. You can deny the permission and choose a city manually instead.

Data stored on your device. Your bookmarks, last-read position, favourite surahs, prayer tracker, tasbeeh counts, saved duas, your last detected location (coordinates and city name, so prayer times still work if GPS is unavailable), reading preferences (theme, font size, language, reciter) and adhan notification settings are stored locally on your device. We cannot see this data. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Notifications and alarms. If you enable adhan notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your device at prayer times. These are created on your device and are not sent to us.

6. Children

The App contains religious content suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not store your personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove data held on your device, clear the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App.

8. Security

Requests to the services above use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security.
